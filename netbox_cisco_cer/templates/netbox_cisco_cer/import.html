How syncing works
  • National ERL export: in CER Admin, open the National E911 ERL list and click Export. Upload the CSV unchanged.
  • Matching: ERLs are matched by name, so re-importing a newer export updates them in place. New ERLs are matched to a Site by name prefix (ADMN 0-2 becomes ADMN), then by street address. ERLs with no match are imported without a site.
  • ELIN tracking sheet: loads the ELIN pool. The New ERL form then suggests the next Available number, and duplicate ELINs are blocked.
  • Start with Dry run to preview the changes. Nothing is saved until you untick it.
